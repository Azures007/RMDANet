RMDANet v7.4-p4p2
====================
Built: 2026-11-17
Base: v7.3-slim11 (11 weighted loss sub-terms, 4 loss families)

Changes in v7.4 (validated on Pavia/Houston/HyRANK x 3 seeds):
  - P4: source-prototype high-confidence filter (P4_FILTER=0.98 default).
  - P2: inference-time target-prior calibration via
    scripts/final_eval_locked.py --prior-calib (alpha=0.5, smooth=0.5, floor=0.02).
  - 3-seed results: Pavia 84.94 (+1.60), Houston 78.95 (+0.32), HyRANK 62.02 (+2.55)
    vs v7.3-slim11 baseline.
  - No new loss terms and no new reliability components.

Rejected experiment (do not enable): P6 weak-class low-frequency augmentation.
  Its apparent +1.53 on Pavia came from applying the augmentation at evaluation
  time using target GT labels; training-only P6 lowers Pavia to 85.08 (-0.95).
  P6 code remains default-off and is gated to training augmentation only.
